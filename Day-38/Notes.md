✅ Applied backtracking by restoring the cell after exploring possibilities
✅ Improved understanding of pruning invalid paths early

🧠 Backtracking Pattern:

Choose a path
⬇️
Check validity
⬇️
Mark visited
⬇️
Explore recursively
⬇️
Undo the choice (Backtrack)

This problem strengthened my foundation in recursive problem solving and showed how the same backtracking template can be applied to different scenarios.
